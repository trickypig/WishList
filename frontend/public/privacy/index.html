Trickypig Wish List

Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This policy explains what happens when you use the Trickypig Wish List Chrome extension, sign in to Wish, and save or share items in your account.

When you open the extension

Clicking Add to Wish lets the extension read the current tab’s address, title, and product information, including a price and image address when available. It reads these details on your device to fill in the form. Its access is limited to the tab you invoke it on. It does not collect your browsing history.

If the form shows a product photo, your browser requests it from the website hosting the image. That host can receive your IP address and the requested image address as soon as the preview loads, even if you close the form without saving.

When you sign in and save

The extension sends your email address and password to wish.trickypig.com over HTTPS to sign you in. Wish stores your email, display name, and a password hash. The extension keeps a sign-in token and your account ID in local Chrome storage so it can retrieve your wishlists and save items for you. It also remembers your last-used list on that device.

When you click Save, the extension sends Wish the selected list, item name, store hostname, full page address, and any price or image address you include. A saved link includes its query parameters, which can identify a product variant or contain information specific to your visit. Wish stores the item in your account so you can use it across devices.

Who can see your items

Your list’s sharing settings determine which family members can see its items and open the saved links. A list created in the extension is shared with all your Wish families. You can change sharing in Wish. When someone views a product image or opens a shopping link, their browser contacts the corresponding website.

The extension uses data obtained through Chrome APIs to help you save products to Wish. Its use and transfer of that data follow the Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements. The extension does not sell browsing data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can change the item name or price, remove the image, or close the popup before saving. After saving, you can edit or delete the item or list in Wish. Signing out of the extension clears its local sign-in session. Removing the extension clears its local preferences. Items already saved stay in your Wish account until you delete them or their list.

Privacy questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about your account data, email support@trickypig.com.
